## Model Answer

Brand 已確認嘅外部連結及 Integration 欄位如下：

- **Website (English)**｜選填｜建議／預設：Website URL｜用途：前台網站連結
- **Website (Local Language)**｜選填｜建議／預設：中文網站 URL｜用途：中文前台網站連結
- **Reservation Methods**｜選填｜建議／預設：Storellet / Inline / Gulu 等｜用途：訂座入口，需與訂座平台integrate
- **External Links**｜選填｜建議／預設：Key、Sort、Text、Type、URL、Icon｜用途：前台 social / external link
- **Email**｜選填｜建議／預設：CS / merchant email｜用途：聯絡我們：聯絡email
- **Facebook**｜選填｜建議／預設：Facebook URL｜用途：Brand social link
- **Instagram**｜選填｜建議／預設：Instagram URL｜用途：Brand social link
- **URL**｜選填｜建議／預設：URL｜用途：Brand website link
- **About Us**｜選填｜建議／預設：About Us URL｜用途：About Us 連結
- **How To Use**｜選填｜建議／預設：How To Use URL｜用途：教學連結
- **Eshop URL**｜選填｜建議／預設：Eshop URL｜用途：Eshop連結
- **Enable Qonbay Integration**｜選填｜建議／預設：Untick｜用途：/
- **Push Notification To White Label App Ids Refer To Master Brand:**｜選填｜建議／預設：Untick｜用途：/
- **Enable Shopline Integration**｜選填｜建議／預設：Shopline eshop url＋Shopline API token｜用途：Shopline Integration
- **Inline Company Id**｜選填｜建議／預設：Inline Company Id｜用途：Inline Integration

## 注意事項

- Reservation Methods 需要同相應訂座平台 integrate。
- 來源對 Enable Qonbay Integration 及 Push Notification To White Label App Ids Refer To Master Brand 的用途只列為「/」，暫時唔應自行推斷。
