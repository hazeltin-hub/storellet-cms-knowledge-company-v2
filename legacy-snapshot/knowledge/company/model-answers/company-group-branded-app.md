## Model Answer

如 Company Group 用於 Branded App，以下 5 個欄位標示為 Branded APP 必填：

- **Group Logo**｜Branded APP必填｜建議／預設：Group Logo｜用途：Branded App 內顯示
- **Background Image**｜Branded APP必填｜建議／預設：Group背景圖｜用途：Branded App 餐廳頁面顯示
- **Email**｜Branded APP必填｜建議／預設：CS / merchant email｜用途：聯絡我們：聯絡email
- **Branded App Terms and Conditions**｜Branded APP必填｜建議／預設：HTML/TXT 格式｜用途：Branded App T&C
- **Privacy Policy**｜Branded APP必填｜建議／預設：HTML/TXT 格式｜用途：私隱政策
