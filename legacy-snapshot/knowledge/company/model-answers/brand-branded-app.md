## Model Answer

如 Brand 用於 Branded App，以下 5 個欄位標示為 Branded APP 必填：

- **Enable Brand Profile**｜Branded APP必填｜建議／預設：Tick｜用途：啟用品牌profile，不跟Storellet profile
- **Thumbnails**｜Branded APP必填｜建議／預設：圖片｜用途：Branded App 品牌圖
- **App Display Method**｜Branded APP必填｜建議／預設：Show on both｜用途：控制brand是否在Storellet app出現
- **Push Notification Method**｜Branded APP必填｜建議／預設：Push to both｜用途：push notification 是否push 到storellet和branded app
- **Branded App App ID**｜Branded APP必填｜建議／預設：APP Config ID｜用途：控制出現在iOS/AOS APP
